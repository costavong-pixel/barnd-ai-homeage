What a licence means

One-site plans

The current public SEO Pro and Complete presentation covers one WordPress website. Site identity and Pro authentication are bound to the activated installation.

Agency / multi-site

The platform has site-limit and agency groundwork, but public agency pricing should remain closed until the final Paddle plan mapping and commercial terms are approved.

No ranking guarantee

A licence gives you product capabilities and updates for the covered plan. It does not guarantee rankings, indexing, traffic, AI citations, malware removal, uninterrupted service or compatibility with every WordPress stack.
