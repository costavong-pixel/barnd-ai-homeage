FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

Still deciding? Here is how the product, plans, safety, and customer delivery fit together.

What is Barnd AI SEO?

Barnd AI SEO is a WordPress SEO plugin with local tools and connected AI assistance. It finds SEO problems, prepares practical fixes, and keeps review, verification, rollback, and monitoring visible.

What is the Founding 500 deal?

The first 500 paying customers receive lifetime access under the plan they purchase. SEO Pro is US$89 one-time for 1 site or US$199 one-time for 10 sites. No annual renewal applies to those customers.

Is SEO Free actually free?

Yes. SEO Free is US$0 and provides a local WordPress foundation. You do not need a Pro account or AI provider key for its included tools.

How do I get Pro after purchase?

Paddle sends the receipt. Use the transaction ID and purchase email on the secure downloads page to receive a fresh entitled link and private activation key.

Can I review AI changes first?

Yes. Review-first mode shows the exact source, current value, proposed value, reason, and status before apply. You can edit or reject a draft.

What can automation change?

You choose the scan rhythm and eligible safe checks. Content additions, FAQ work, and other review-required changes remain in the queue until approved.

Can I roll back a change?

Yes. Barnd stores bounded prior state for each applied change and restores only that change. It does not use a whole-site restore for routine undo.

Does Barnd guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. It improves the SEO work and signals you control. Search engines and answer systems decide indexing, rankings, traffic, and citations.

Where can I get help?

Use Support with your site URL, plugin versions, and a non-secret diagnostic code. Never send passwords, payment details, activation keys, or private signing keys.
