GEO guide

Choosing a WordPress SEO plugin for AI search.

Answer systems need clear, consistent information. A plugin can improve the signals you control; it cannot guarantee that a model will cite or recommend you.

Make the entity clear

State who you are, what you offer, where you operate, and which pages explain each service. Use consistent Organization, Product, SoftwareApplication, LocalBusiness, and Article details where they genuinely fit.

Make answers useful

Helpful visible FAQs

Answer real customer questions in visible content. Keep any FAQ structured data aligned with what visitors can read.

Strong page relationships

Connect your product, feature, pricing, docs, security, and support pages with meaningful internal links.

Fresh content signals

Monitor titles, descriptions, images, links, schema, and local information after the site changes.

Honest measurement

Use audits and reports to see what changed, but do not turn a score into a promise of AI placement.
