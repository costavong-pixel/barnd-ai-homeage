Privacy

Prompt Overlay Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Prompt Overlay is provided by Barnd AI.

Data collection

Prompt Overlay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Your scripts

Scripts, text formatting, and reading preferences remain on your device. Prompt Overlay does not upload or store your scripts on Barnd AI servers.

Camera and recording

Prompt Overlay opens your phone’s normal camera app so you can read a floating prompt while recording. Prompt Overlay does not access your camera or microphone, and the floating prompt is not included in your saved video.

Permissions

The app may request permission to display over other apps so it can show a user-requested floating prompt. It may also use a foreground service and notifications to keep that prompt available while you record. These permissions are used only for the teleprompter feature.

Ads and analytics

Prompt Overlay contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Barnd AI does not receive or store your payment-card information. Google handles payment information under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@barndai.com.
