Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Local-first product behavior

Barnd AI Shield performs its standard security checks inside the WordPress site where it is installed. Its standard local checks do not send scan results to Barnd AI.

Information we receive

We receive information you choose to send us, such as support emails and product questions. Paddle processes checkout and payment information as merchant of record; we do not receive your full payment-card details.

How we use information

We use support information to respond to you, troubleshoot the product, prevent abuse, and improve our software. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and sharing

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for support, legal, and operational purposes. We share information only with service providers needed to deliver support or payments, when required by law, or with your consent.
