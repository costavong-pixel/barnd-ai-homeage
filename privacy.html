Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Local-first Free features

Barnd AI SEO Free performs its included SEO tools inside the WordPress site where it is installed. Local audits, metadata controls, schema, sitemap/LLMs.txt tools, image ALT review, redirects, 404 monitoring, internal-link review, Google Performance import and the local AI Discoverability snapshot do not require an external AI provider.

Optional site registration and Pro activation

If an administrator chooses to register or activate a connected Barnd feature, the plugin may send the site URL, local/site identifiers, product and version information, WordPress/PHP environment information, entitlement/activation data and other connection metadata required to operate that feature. Customer credentials used for WordPress hosting are not required by the normal activation flow.

Pro AI actions

When an entitled Pro AI feature is deliberately invoked, the content and context needed for that action may be sent to Barnd AI SEO API and from there to the configured AI provider. The upstream AI provider credential is stored on the Barnd API server and is not delivered to customer WordPress installations.

Security and authentication data

Pro may process installation/site identifiers, public signing-key material, key versions, request identifiers, nonces, timestamps, entitlement/capability state, rate/cost data and security/audit outcomes. WordPress private signing keys are intended to remain on the customer installation rather than being stored as reusable private keys on the Barnd API.

Support information

We receive information you choose to send us, such as support emails, diagnostic summaries and product questions. Do not send passwords, private signing keys, database credentials, payment-card numbers or provider API keys in support messages.

Payments

When commercial checkout opens, the configured payment provider will process payment details. Barnd AI should receive the transaction/customer/entitlement information needed to fulfil the purchase, but not your full payment-card credentials. The final payment-provider identity and checkout disclosures will be confirmed when production billing is activated.

How we use information

We use the information described above to provide requested features, authenticate sites, enforce entitlements, prevent abuse, control provider usage/cost, troubleshoot problems, provide updates and support, improve reliability, and meet legal or operational obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and sharing

We retain operational, entitlement, security and support data only as reasonably needed for product operation, security, support, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. We share information only with service providers needed to deliver requested functionality, process payments, operate infrastructure or comply with law.
