Privacy

We collect what the product needs, not everything it can.

This summary explains the main data boundaries for the website and Barnd AI SEO. For a specific request, contact support.

Website and checkout

Website hosting may receive normal request metadata. Paddle processes payment and provides transaction and customer details needed for delivery, entitlement, and support. Barnd does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Downloads and licensing

The secure download flow uses a transaction ID and purchase email to verify entitlement and issue a short-lived link. License and installation records are used to authorize the plan and site limit.

Connected SEO workflows

When Pro is enabled, the service receives the minimum site and request information needed to authenticate the installation, enforce capability and usage limits, and return an AI recommendation or report. Provider credentials remain server-side. Do not place secrets in page content or draft prompts.

Retention and support

We retain operational records for licensing, security, billing reconciliation, support, and audit purposes for as long as reasonably necessary. Ask support@barndai.com about a specific record.

Your choices

You can use SEO Free locally without a connected AI account. You can choose review-first mode, disable automation, reject drafts, and request support for account or licensing questions.

Contact

Email support@barndai.com. Never send passwords, private keys, payment-card details, provider credentials, or raw activation keys.
