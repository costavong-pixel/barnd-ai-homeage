Install

  1. Download the approved ZIP once the controlled download link is enabled.
  2. In WordPress, open Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin.
  3. Upload the ZIP and activate Barnd AI SEO.
  4. Open Barnd AI SEO and run Setup, then review the SEO Audit.
