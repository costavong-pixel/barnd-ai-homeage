Refund Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Controlled rollout: public production checkout is not yet open. The final payment-provider/refund mechanics will be verified during the commercial E2E before general sales begin.

14-day request window

Unless different mandatory consumer rights apply, you may request a refund within 14 days of the initial purchase if the purchased Barnd AI product does not work as described for your supported WordPress setup. We may ask for reasonable non-secret diagnostic details so we can understand the issue.

How to request a refund

Email support@barndai.com with the email address used at checkout, the affected product/site, and the payment transaction/order reference. Never send payment-card data, passwords, API keys or private signing keys.

What happens to a refunded Pro entitlement

A full refund may cause the corresponding paid entitlement to be revoked or moved to the appropriate non-paid state. Local Free functionality remains governed by the Free product; paid Pro-connected capabilities require a valid entitlement.

Abuse and exceptions

Refunds may be declined where a request is fraudulent or abusive, material information needed to identify the transaction is missing, or a refund is not available under the applicable checkout/provider rules. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Payment provider

The final production payment provider and transaction workflow will be displayed at checkout. Approved refunds are returned through the payment provider to the applicable original payment method where supported.
